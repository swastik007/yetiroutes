## 💼 BUSINESS MODEL OVERVIEW

### Revenue Streams

#### 1. Trek Packages (Primary - 85% of revenue)
**Product Categories:**
- **Budget Treks:** $600-1,200 (Poon Hill, Langtang Valley)
- **Mid-Range Treks:** $1,200-2,000 (ABC, EBC, Annapurna Circuit)
- **Premium Treks:** $2,000-4,000 (Manaslu, Three Passes, Island Peak)
- **Custom/Private Treks:** $150-250/day (flexible itineraries)

**Average Transaction Value:** $1,299 (weighted average)

**Booking Cycle:**
- Lead generation → Inquiry → Quote → Booking (2-3 weeks average)
- Booking to Departure: 2-6 months advance booking
- Season concentration: 70% bookings in Mar-May, Sep-Nov

#### 2. Activities & Side Trips (10% of revenue)
- Mountain climbing
- Helicopter tours
- Cultural experiences
- Wildlife safaris
- Peak climbing courses

**Average Value:** $300-1,500 per activity

#### 3. Add-Ons & Services (5% of revenue)
- Gear rental ($50-150 per trek)
- Airport transfers ($25-50)
- Extra accommodation in Kathmandu
- Travel insurance referral commissions
- Permit processing fees

### Cost Structure

**Fixed Costs (Monthly):**
- Office rent in Kathmandu: $200-300
- Staff salaries (admin): $400-600
- Website hosting: $20-50
- Phone/internet: $30-50
- **Total Monthly Fixed:** ~$650-1,000

**Variable Costs (Per Trek):**
- Guide salary: $30/day × 14 days = $420
- Porter salary: $25/day × 14 days = $350
- Permits: $30-50
- Domestic flights (if applicable): $350-400
- Teahouse accommodation: $5-10/night × nights
- Meals: Included in teahouse costs
- Transportation (Kathmandu-trailhead): $50-100
- Insurance for staff: $20-30
- **Total Variable Cost (EBC example):** ~$1,200-1,500

**Gross Margin:**
- Trek Price: $1,800
- Variable Costs: $1,300
- **Gross Profit:** $500 (28% margin)
- After Fixed Costs: $500 - $100 (allocated fixed) = $400 net per booking

**Break-Even Analysis:**
- Fixed costs: $850/month
- Net profit per booking: $400
- **Break-even:** 2-3 bookings per month
- **Target:** 10-12 bookings per month for sustainable growth

## ⚠️ RISK ASSESSMENT

### High-Impact Risks

#### 1. **SEO Competition**
**Risk:** Established competitors already rank well, hard to break through
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Impact:** High (directly affects traffic)

**Mitigation:**
- Focus on long-tail keywords first (less competition)
- Target region-specific content (Langtang, Manaslu vs Everest)
- Build content moat (50+ articles, competitors have 10-20)
- Better content quality (2,000+ words vs 500-word competitor articles)

**Contingency:**
- If organic growth slow, allocate $2,000/month to Google Ads
- Partner with travel bloggers for backlinks
- Invest in Ahrefs/SEMrush for competitive insights

#### 2. **Seasonality**
**Risk:** 70% of bookings concentrated in 4 months (Mar-May, Sep-Nov)
**Likelihood:** High (certainty)
**Impact:** Medium (cash flow management)

**Mitigation:**
- Promote off-season treks (Lower Langtang in winter, Dolpo in monsoon)
- Offer early bird discounts for peak season
- Build cash reserves during peak months
- Diversify to year-round activities (cultural tours, climbing courses)

**Contingency:**
- Reduce fixed costs during off-season
- Guides work other jobs in off-season (acceptable in industry)
- Offer payment plans (50% deposit, 50% later)

#### 3. **Content Creation Backlog**
**Risk:** Can't produce 20-30 blog posts fast enough
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Impact:** Medium (delayed SEO results)

**Mitigation:**
- Hire freelance writer ($50-100/article) if needed
- Use AI assistance for drafts (Claude/GPT-4)
- Prioritize top 10 articles first (80/20 rule)
- Extend timeline to 6 months vs 3 months (acceptable)

**Contingency:**
- Focus on optimizing existing 11 posts first (easier, faster)
- Accept slower growth trajectory
- Increase paid ad spend to compensate

#### 4. **Review Generation**
**Risk:** Hard to get first 20-50 reviews without customers
**Likelihood:** Medium
**Impact:** Medium (social proof critical for conversions)

**Mitigation:**
- Ask past customers for reviews (if any)
- Offer small incentive (5% discount on next trek) for honest review
- Use TripAdvisor, Google, Facebook reviews
- Display testimonials from email/WhatsApp

**Contingency:**
- Partner with micro-influencers for honest reviews
- Offer 1-2 free treks to travel bloggers in exchange for review
- Use trust badges (certifications, associations) as substitute

### Medium-Impact Risks

#### 5. **Payment Gateway Issues**
**Risk:** Stripe/Khalti integration delays or payment failures
**Likelihood:** Low
**Impact:** Medium

**Mitigation:**
- Accept bank transfers as backup
- Manual invoice processing until automation ready
- WhatsApp as primary contact (easier for customers)

#### 6. **Website Downtime**
**Risk:** Server issues, hosting problems
**Likelihood:** Low
**Impact:** High (lost bookings)

**Mitigation:**
- Use reliable hosting (A2 Hosting, SiteGround)
- Set up monitoring (UptimeRobot - free)
- Have backup/restore process
- Daily backups automated

#### 7. **Google Algorithm Changes**
**Risk:** Algorithm update tanks rankings
**Likelihood:** Medium (happens 2-4 times/year)
**Impact:** High if hit

**Mitigation:**
- Follow white-hat SEO only (no black-hat tactics)
- Diversify traffic sources (organic, paid, social, referrals)
- Build brand (direct traffic less affected by algorithm)
- Monitor Search Console for manual actions

### Low-Impact Risks

- Domain expiration (set auto-renew)
- Technical bugs (test thoroughly, monitoring)
- Staff turnover (maintain pool of qualified guides)
- Competitor copying (build brand loyalty, reviews)
